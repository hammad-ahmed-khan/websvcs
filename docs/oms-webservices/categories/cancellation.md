### Cross-cutting notes

- **XX_REFUND_REQUEST_PKG.create_xrr_refund_request** is called **only** from `Hybris_Cancellation` (`HybrisCancellationServImpl.insertDetailInMuleTableProcedure`, line 633-634). `update_xrr_processing_status`, `update_xrr_Sadad_status`, `update_gl_accounting` are not invoked anywhere in this cluster (presumably called from Sadad/GL settlement batch modules outside scope).
- **XXHDB_CORE_PKG.cancel_booking_small** is called only from `oms-cancellation` (`CancelOrderDAO.cancelOddBooking`, line 702), gated on `deliveryMode=='ODDSMALL'`.
- **OMS_WEBSERVICE_URI_DETAIL** is read only from `oms-cancellation` (`CancelOrderDAO.saveWSPublishReq`, line 666), to resolve the `WEB_SERVICE_ID` for `'SOA_CO_STATUS_UPDATE'` when queueing a Siebel-notification republish row into `OMS_REPUBLISH_DATA`.
- None of the 5 modules reference `OMSSUB_ASNOUT`, `OMSSUB_RECEIVING`, `OMS_ASNOUT_WS_INVOKER`, `OMS_RECEIPT_WS_INVOKER`, `OMS_SOS_WS_INVOKER`, `OMS_PENDRETURN_WS_INVOKER`, `OMS_MSG_WS_PUBLISHER`, or `XX_DLV_ADDRESS_UPDATE` — those PL/SQL packages belong to other business clusters.
- **Architecture pattern**: `OMSCOCancellation` (JAX-WS/EJB, older) and `oms-cancellation` (Spring REST/JDBC, newer) implement functionally overlapping "cancel a customer order" logic against the same schema. `Hybris_Cancellation` and `oms-cancellation-job` both call into `oms-cancellation`'s `/omscancellation` REST endpoint rather than the older SOAP service, suggesting `oms-cancellation` is the currently-active implementation and `OMSCOCancellation` is legacy/being phased out.
- **Transaction safety gap**: both `oms-cancellation` (`CancelOrderDAO`) and `Hybris_Cancellation` (`HybrisCancellationServImpl`) carry `@Transactional` annotations with **no transaction-manager bean configured** in their respective Spring XML contexts — multi-statement operations are not atomic; a mid-sequence failure leaves partial writes committed.
